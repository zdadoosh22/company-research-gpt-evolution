## Short-horizon research addendum to develop

For every proposed opportunity, distinguish three records:

1. **Company judgement:** business economics, financial resilience, material facts and opposing evidence. Preserve the methodology/run version, source locations and review identity. A partial pilot is still partial.
2. **Portfolio decision:** why this opportunity is preferable to cash/alternatives over the stated interval; entry evidence and price ceiling; cost sensitivity; proposed commitment and reserved cash; scenario exposure and interaction with other sleeve positions. Cost basis does not change intrinsic value or business forecasts.
3. **Execution instruction:** exact listing/class, shares, trading currency/unit, GBP ceiling including estimated costs, order type/time-in-force, quote timestamp, expiry and cancellation responsibilities. A research opinion or allocation proposal is not an executable instruction.

The short-term layer must explain what might change market expectations, when that evidence can arrive, what is already expected and why the chosen horizon is plausible. It must not simply shorten a five-year valuation into a predicted near-term target. Maintain the event calendar, overnight/earnings exposure, trading-session boundaries and a response window compatible with delayed phone reading. Reject opportunities requiring an immediate response when the user may be unavailable.

## Exit comparison and performance protocol

Register each opportunity before its outcome is known. Compare these exit arms on identical entries and quantities:

| Arm | Rule to freeze before the opportunity | Failure to test |
|---|---|---|
| Price-risk exit | Evidence-based trigger and chosen stop type | Temporary dip, overnight gap, halt, partial fill and stop-limit nonexecution |
| Thesis exit | Specific contemporaneous evidence that invalidates the expected mechanism | Delayed discovery, disputed evidence and hours/days of user response delay |
| Time exit | Dated event window/deadline or explicit evidence required to extend it | Catalyst delay, moving deadlines to hide losses and continued stale assumptions |

Taking profits after favourable repricing, trimming concentration and exiting deterioration are different decisions. A profitable position may warrant exit because its prospective opportunity has weakened; a losing position may have an intact thesis. Neither entry cost nor an arbitrary gain/loss percentage establishes that judgement.

Paper comparison must reserve actual hypothetical sleeve cash/shares: no overlapping unlimited positions or imaginary use of unrelated ISA funds. Keep early exit proceeds in cash until the common comparison deadline to isolate exit effects; test reinvestment separately. Record issuance, acknowledgement and realistic execution time. Use contemporaneous executable prices where available; a touched limit is not proof of a fill. Missing data, uncertainty, standing orders and partial fills stay visible.

Measure GBP cash plus investments, realised/unrealised gain, actual costs, drawdown, concentration, turnover, holding time and delayed/missed execution. Show gross and net outcomes. Select cash and an appropriate investable passive benchmark before results, aligning dates, capital and cost/FX assumptions. Record all tested variants and rejected opportunities. Revised rules begin new prospective cohorts; do not repeatedly optimise against the same completed trades or retroactively reconstruct successful recommendations.

There is no annualised performance target, assumed win rate or selected numerical stop in this document. The fourteen-day operational trial cannot establish a strategy that holds for months. Establish the required opportunity sample and evaluation questions before observing performance; no model-issued profitability certificate substitutes for that evidence.

## Order and cost mechanics

Buy limits can bound the share price, but do not fix total GBP cost when currency conversion or fees vary. Reserve a cost-aware GBP amount and inspect the broker preview. Sell limits support price-based profit-taking, with possible nonexecution. Stops turn into market orders after triggering, so gaps can produce substantially worse sale prices. Stop-limits restrict execution price but can leave the investment unsold. [SEC order guide](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/how-stock-markets-work/types-orders), [Trading 212 stop-limit guide](https://helpcentre.trading212.com/hc/en-us/articles/360007081297-Stop-Limit-Orders).

Trading 212 documents fractional market, limit, stop and stop-limit support for most eligible Invest/ISA instruments; pending fractional orders use share quantity. Instrument availability and the actual order preview still require checking. Do not presume linked stop/profit orders automatically cancel one another or reserve the same shares safely; verify this before proposing simultaneous orders. [Fractional orders](https://helpcentre.trading212.com/hc/en-us/articles/9511997937437-Can-I-trade-with-fractional-shares).

The documented FX fee is 0.15% for orders in another currency; the broker preview reports it. Corporate-event payments such as dividends do not incur that FX fee under the cited guidance. Taxes, market spread, currency movement and other applicable charges remain separate. Check actual instrument costs; do not apply a generic tax or treat headline commission-free trading as costless. [Trading 212 FX guidance](https://helpcentre.trading212.com/hc/en-us/articles/360018909758-What-is-the-FX-fee-Invest-Stocks-ISA).

The generated `validation/short-term-simulations.json` illustrates contrasting fictional paths and FX-style fee drag. Its triggers and paths are test values, not chosen trading policy. It is neither a historical backtest nor an estimate of likely profit, and it does not reproduce a broker's matching engine.
