## What would warrant the first real instruction?

A complete, reviewed opportunity dossier must justify the company judgement and short-term mechanism using available dated evidence. A separate portfolio decision must obey a documented, tested policy and verified sleeve cash/order state. Execution must use a verified quote, correct listing/currency/unit, feasible order and cost-aware commitment. Phone delivery, current-status refresh, expiry and cancellation responsibilities must work with delayed human execution. Missing material evidence must defeat the instruction, even when a favourable scenario looks attractive.

The authorised design's 15-minute refreshed confirmation is a communication safeguard. It is a proposed operating parameter from that design, not evidence that the thesis or price remains valid for fifteen minutes; its behaviour still needs validation against the eventual quote/strategy workflow. The current version never creates such a confirmation.

Acceptance does not mean proving that future returns will be positive. A later live sleeve would remain an explicitly uncertain experiment. It does mean that the proposed decision has a defensible evidence basis, its costs and failure cases are understood, and its controls work. Neither user willingness to lose £300 nor a model's confidence replaces those requirements.
