## Warrants and acquisition shares must follow the same business case

June rounded common1,632m and July29 cover actual1,632.475042m are different dated observations. June weighted diluted EPS1,659m is a period average including employee equity effects, not current total claims. Notes12 states no OpenAI/Meta warrant shares vested or became exercisable at June27. That is not an October vesting assertion.

Each customer warrant permits up to160m shares atUSD0.01; two maxima320m produce only3.2m exercise cash. Purchase milestones, stock-price/performance conditions and further technical/commercial conditions govern exercise. Using the July29 common observation solely as a **dated denominator diagnostic**, hypothetical full exercise gives1,952.475042m total; old holders retain about83.61%. It is not a probability-weighted current dilution forecast. Original June-rounded19.61% addition/16.39% ownership-reduction calculation remains valid on its own basis; do not overwrite it with a supposedly more current October denominator.

Let N be dated existing claims, W the additional exercised warrants and S the net additional acquisition/employee shares. Holding total distributable economics constant, old-owner share isN/(N+W+S). To preserve per-share economics, total distributable value must grow by at least(W+S)/N, subject to any exercise proceeds and acquired cash/claims consistently included. This is a mathematical ownership identity, **not** a forecast that customers destroy or create value. Greater customer purchases can deliver gross profit and cash while earning warrants; weaker demand may prevent vesting but strand commitments. These outcomes must be coupled, not modelled as independently best-case revenue and zero dilution. Do not fully dilute and also charge full warrant fair value for the same claim without coherent settlement accounting.

Add future acquired cash/expense/benefit and shares together; all-stock does not mean costless. Actual closing, adjustment and performance remain unknown.
