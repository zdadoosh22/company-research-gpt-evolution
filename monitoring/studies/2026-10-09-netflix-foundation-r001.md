## Persistence and expectations

Independent Nielsen measurement and competing streaming releases challenge an assumption that growing Netflix usage itself proves customer retention, pricing acceptance or profitable ad conversion. US TV viewing is not global paid membership, device-wide engagement or advertising-sales measurement; competitor reports are interested sources with different fiscal periods and segment definitions. The source note gives actual dates and specific opposing observations, including its newer July reading. No peer-margin average is used as a Netflix forecast.

The provider annual operating-margin display is close to July's issuer baseline. Its adjusted EPS and FCF lack receipt/tax reconciliation; no matched quarterly surprise hurdle is available. Delivering an already-guided plan is not new evidence of unexpected earnings. A persistent revision would need repeatable pricing/ad contribution net of retention/churn and sustainable content/cash costs, measured against a defined comparable baseline. No demand dataset supplies those economics alone.
