SR-FINRA-2026-021
Proposed Rule Change to Temporarily Pause Assessment of the Trading Activity Fee
Financial Industry Regulatory Authority, Inc. (“FINRA”) is filing with the Securities and Exchange Commission (“SEC” or “Commission”) a proposed rule change to temporarily pause assessment of the Trading Activity Fee (“TAF”) for three months, for transactions from October 1, 2026 through December 31, 2026.
