Can I choose the currency in which to buy/sell assets for Invest accounts?
With the Trading 212 Invest account, you have the flexibility to choose which currency you use for your trades. This allows you to manage your funds more efficiently and reduce costs.
📄 Note
When investing, your capital is at risk. A FX fee applies when converting funds. Other fees may apply.
How do I select the currency for my order?
Open the instrument you wish to trade.
Tap Buy or Sell.
On the order screen, tap on the currency displayed next to the price.
Select your preferred currency from the list.
Can I set a default currency for my trades?
Yes. You can set a default currency to make placing orders quicker and easier.
To set a default currency for your trades:
Tap ☰.
Select Settings.
Select Currency options.
Are there any limitations?
Pies & AutoInvest: Currently, Pies support only your account’s primary currency. If a Pie contains assets denominated in a different currency, an automatic conversion will take place and the standard FX fee will apply.
ISA and CFD accounts: Multi-currency order selection is available only for Invest accounts. ISA and CFD accounts use the primary account currency for all transactions.
