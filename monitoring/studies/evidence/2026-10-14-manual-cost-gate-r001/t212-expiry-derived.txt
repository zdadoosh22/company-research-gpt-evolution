Can I set my orders to expire?
Yes. When setting a pending order (Stop, Limit or Stop-Limit) on the Invest or Stocks ISA, you have two options for setting an expiration:
End of day: The order will automatically expire if not executed by midnight in the time zone of the exchange where the stock is traded.
Never: The order will remain pending unless it is filled or cancelled by yourself.
