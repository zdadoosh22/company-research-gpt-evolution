What are the fees in the Invest, ISAs, and SIPP?
The only fee Trading 212 can charge your Invest, ISAs, and SIPP is the FX Fee.
FX fee: 0.15%
Trading commission: Free
Custody Fee: Free
The other fees related to your trades in such account types are not charged by Trading 212 but by the relevant exchanges and tax authorities:
LSE Stocks
Stamp Duty Reserve Tax is charged at 0.5% on share purchases made for stocks listed on the London Stock Exchange. There is no Stamp Duty charge applied to gilts, bonds or ETFs.
PTM Levy is charged in both cases, on purchase and sale, and it is £1.5 per trade for orders over £10,000.
Note: Most stocks traded on the LSE AIM are exempt from Stamp Duty Reserve Tax.
NYSE Stocks
Transaction Fee (Securities and Exchange Commission) is $0.00206 of the value of the sell order.
FINRA Fee is charged on all covered stock and ETF sales at $0.000195 x quantity sold.
Euronext Paris Stocks
French Financial Transaction Tax (FFT) is 0.4% and is applied to the purchase of shares of French companies with a market cap of over 1 billion.
All of the above fees are presented in the Review order screen right before confirming a trade on the app.
