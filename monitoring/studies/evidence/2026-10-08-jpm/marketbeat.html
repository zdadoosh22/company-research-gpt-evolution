JPMorgan Chase & Co. Earnings Summary

JPMorgan Chase & Co. issued Q2 2026 earnings on July 14, 2026, reporting an EPS of $6.14, which beat analysts' consensus estimates of $5.59 by $0.55. Quarterly revenue rose 27.7% year-over-year to $58.02 billion, above analyst estimates of $50.72 billion. With a trailing EPS of $23.34 and a P/E Ratio of 14.12, JPMorgan Chase & Co.'s earnings are expected to grow 4.43% next year, from $24.16 to $25.23 per share.
