Email Address About Netflix Netflix (NASDAQ:NFLX) (NASDAQ:NFLX) is a global entertainment company that operates a subscription-based streaming service. It offers a broad range of television series, films, documentaries, and other programming, including original productions developed under the Netflix brand and licensed content from third-party studios. The company also provides advertising-supported viewing options in some markets and has expanded into related entertainment categories, including mobile and cloud-based games, live programming, and consumer products associated with selected titles. Netflix distributes its content through internet-connected televisions, mobile devices, computers, and other compatible platforms. Netflix was founded in 1997 by Reed Hastings and Marc Randolph as a DVD-by-mail rental business before transitioning to online streaming. The company launched its streaming service in 2007 and subsequently expanded internationally, serving members across more than 190 countries and territories. Its content and service offerings vary by market because of regional licensing arrangements and local production activities. Netflix is led by co-chief executive officers Ted Sarandos and Greg Peters. Sarandos oversees the company’s content operations, while Peters has been involved in product, technology, and business operations. The company is headquartered in Los Gatos, California.
