## Research consequence and next test

Do not silently normalize compensation using whichever table gives the most favourable result. Do not explain reserve differences by assumed foreign exchange or acquisitions. Obtain the accessible issuer release, complete tables and any source accounting bridges; compare scope and period before claiming equality. If differences remain unexplained, keep them visible and assess whether any proposed decision actually depends on a normalized owner-cash estimate. No such decision is accepted here.

For future forecasts, model compensation cost and ownership consistently, reserve liabilities with their claim-payment timing, and insurance collateral separately from discretionary cash. Subtracting one working-capital change is a sensitivity rather than a sustainable cash estimate. A conservative bound requires evidence about exposures and cash uses; it cannot be created by arbitrary haircuts. Material future valuation changes require independent review, including these contrary observations.
