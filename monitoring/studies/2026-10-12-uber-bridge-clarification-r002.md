# Uber bridge repayment clarification r002 — 12 October 2026

Scoped clarification of the bridge paragraph in `2026-10-12-uber-funding-r001.md` and its web fact register. Preserve those frozen originals. This note does not change purchase-proceeds arithmetic, coupon dates, cash-definition observations or the withholding of current funding/entry conclusions. Independent correction review pending.

The [amended bridge agreement](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1543151/000155278126000414/e26328_ex10-2.htm), filed 7 August 2026, must be read across repayment and commitment reduction provisions rather than treating them as interchangeable:

- Section 2.05(b)(i) governs mandatory loan prepayment from defined net proceeds after closing. Its final sentence defers required prepayment before the defined Final Settlement Date. A generic three-business-day receipt rule alone is therefore incomplete.
- Section 2.05(b)(ii) preserves the Restricted Net Cash Proceeds exception for foreign-subsidiary proceeds under the stated tax/law/organizational restrictions and determination conditions. Such proceeds cannot simply be treated as immediately available to repay parent debt.
- Section 2.06(b)(iii) distinguishes preclosing and postclosing commitment reductions, excludes Restricted Net Cash Proceeds where specified, and imposes preclosing blocked-account/Cash Confirmation Agreement treatment. Section 2.06(c) specifies allocation first to outstanding loans then commitments.

Thus no actual repayment date, unrestricted-cash release or current bridge balance is inferred from the September issuance. The relevant defined dates, proceeds classifications, restrictions and actual receipt/account conditions remain needed. A reduction in undrawn commitment is not a cash repayment; borrowing receipt is not earnings; nominal notes principal is not necessarily qualifying net proceeds.
