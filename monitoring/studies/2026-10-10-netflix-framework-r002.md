## What this computes, and what it withholds

The gate passes for a **limited conditional economic framework**. It computes the present value, at a **pro forma 1 January2027 reference**, of ten years of modeled cash available to the existing common claims plus a mature cash franchise, after scheduled corporate debt payments. It excludes any opening surplus-cash value and excludes exercise proceeds. Consequently its per-claim figures are **cash-franchise components under explicit conditions**, not total equity values, October intrinsic values, target prices or entry ceilings. January balances and share count have not happened and are not known. There is no claimed return or comparison to a current executable price.

The approved five-year default is extended to ten explicit years to let the stronger growth case reach a defensible low-growth state. Case labels describe conditional economics; there are no probabilities and “central” does not mean a statistically expected forecast. Full company approval, portfolio allocation and execution remain withheld. A feasible conditional model does not establish a mispricing or a tradable event.

## Cash bridge, commitments and funding

For each year, coreOI minus cashinterest minus prospective cashtax is followed by content-amortisation addback less planned contentcash, noncontent depreciation less cashcapex, working-capital uses and any recurring acquisition stress. No termination receipt, receipt-tax allocation or Brazil addback is used. Starting GAAPOI retains compensation; CFO's historicalSBC addback is removed for the historical cross-check. Neither receipt nor the85m terminated-financing amortisation is added again.

Planned contentcash is amortisation plus the larger of (a) a fading observed/guided cash-amortisation excess, and (b) cash needed for the assumed content-asset growth. Initial excess ratios are observedH1 16.2009%, guide10%, and observedFY25 7.8253%, respectively. They fade over ten years; the minimum growth funding uses existing content assets times revenue growth, with half that intensity in the upside productivity case. Source actuals permit productivity differences but do not prove the factor0.5. Planned net content assets roll forward by that reinvestment, assuming normalized liability/FX differences zero. Terminal contentcash equals amortisation plusg times the modeled asset stock. Noncontent capital assets roll similarly; terminal netcapex fundsg times their stock, and terminalWC uses depend on incremental revenue. This prevents unsupported terminal growth financed by no investment. The asset roll is a conditional capital-intensity model, not a forecast of reported license liabilities or exact accelerated amortisation.

Known commitments, including recognized liabilities, are paid by planned contentcash and never added as a second claim. The additional unknown-title exposure is tested as: covered0; partial uncovered2bn; full disclosed-range endpoint4bn; and adverse6bn beyond that management range. Uncovered amounts are paid late2027/2028/first-half2029 in25/50/25% proportions, mostly beyond June2027 and within three years of the June2026 note. This schedule is a stated stress assumption, not known title dates. The2029 subtraction is discounted at midyear, not year-end. No tax relief, replacement savings, extra revenue or residual asset value is credited for these incremental payments; they are economic downside costs outside core earnings. This deliberately assumes no value from the extra rights, not a GAAP immediate-expensing assertion. Future ordinary unknown-title procurement is still part of planned contentcash; the stress cannot establish a legal maximum or prove every future commitment covered.

Pro forma Januaryprincipal14.372bn assumes the November2026USD1bn is paid and July's new1bn remains, using then-available cash. It is not confirmed repayment. Euro principal holds JuneFX for modeling; FX changes remain an uncertainty. Repayments2027 1.486bn,2028 3.5bn,2029 4.329bn,2030 2.257bn,2034 1bn and2036 1bn are explicitly deducted; known month maturities determine their discount times. The2054 residual0.8bn and post2036 after-tax interest are separately deducted from terminal value. Tail interest/principal use annual approximation through18years; the final August2054 stub is not a precise coupon schedule. No refinancing or new borrowing funds distributions, and debt is not subtracted again from this directly levered cash component.

Annual funding is tested against an **explicit pro forma opening-liquidity condition9.127910bn**, sourced from June cash/investments, not asserted January cash. Positive available cash can be distributed; a negative year draws that reserve. No opening surplus cash is valued. Actual later buybacks/acquisitions, liquidity restrictions, intrayear timing and currency changes could invalidate the condition. This is an annual conditional funding test, not actual broker/corporate reconciliation or a promise of dividend payments.

Historical reconciliation is inspectable: FY25FCF minusSBC9.092604bn; a specified OI/cashinterest/cashtax/content/netcapex/WC bridge8.291268bn. The0.801336bn difference is fully decomposed into cash-versus-accrued interest−57.899m, interest/other income172.459m, cash-versus-provision tax479.033m, deferred-tax adjustment−442.056m, debtFX72.348m and other-noncash577.451m. No remainder is a hidden recurring-income plug. The central future case is deliberately more conservative than receipt-free management cashguidance; exact tax/timing and noncash conversion prevent claiming a reconstructed management forecast.

The downside cash model is lower because it carries stronger content/acquisition costs; the upside cash model can exceed the historical conversion benchmark because it assumes productivity improves and future taxes/financing fall. ## Expectations and adverse response, applied to the model

The inherited Julyguide and October8 provider snapshots remain frozen; provider forecasts and October9 prices have different timestamps. No new matched quarterlyconsensus is created. The model's near-term cash outcomes differ markedly despite the same approximate revenue seed because taxes, operating conversion, content intensity and acquisition costs differ. A prospective new result must revise these recurring drivers beyond the already expected plan, not merely repeat ads doubling or the termination-relatedFCF increase.

Compare a proposed durable revision against the **full uncovered downside**, not only central earnings: stronger reportedOI can be consumed by incremental content payments, higher tax or reserve use. The numerical model does not turn “newOI” into a stock-price reaction; at a10%earnings improvement/10%multiplecompression, support is0.99, and20%/20% is0.96. No repricing remains possible even for successful business performance. Better efficiency in the upside can be already expected. Correct-but-late recognition may occur outside days-to-months, while delayed manual response can arrive after repricing. Hour/day delays require a new evidence/claim/quote check; gaps/halts prevent assumed fills, and an event postponement invalidates an old event envelope. No actual fill, paper return, alert, order or future observation is fabricated.
