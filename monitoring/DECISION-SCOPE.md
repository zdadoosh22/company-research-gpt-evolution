# Scoped research and delegated decision review

8 October 2026. Extension design addendum; it does not amend the approved general company methodology or approve any current pilot.

The user has delegated design and research decisions for an isolated aggressive £300 sleeve, with manual broker execution and a days-to-months horizon. Sufficiency is the implementer's responsibility. Satisfaction questions, a successful export or a model's confidence cannot discharge that responsibility.

## Decision-sufficient analytical scope

A reviewed scoped company record may support a short-horizon decision without being a complete five-year thesis. It must explicitly cover the security/claims, financial resilience through the holding window, material business drivers, source facts versus management guidance and assumptions, and limitations relevant to the proposed outcome. Source documents must be read with locations and access dates; material unsupported economics block affected conclusions. Existing partial pilots cannot be promoted by relabelling them.

A separate opportunity dossier connects the event to business outcomes, current expectations and a plausible price response. It includes no repricing, adverse surprise, gaps/halts, catalyst delay and correct-but-late outcomes; economically derived ranges; contrary evidence; the reason to prefer the opportunity to cash; and entry, exit and reassessment conditions. A known event alone does not establish a mispricing. Review challenges price response as well as accounting. No finite paper sample proves future profit.

A separate portfolio decision applies explicit experimental capital budgets, current reconciled cash, other exposures, costs and joint adverse scenarios. The execution envelope binds the reviewed versions to exact security, quantity, ceiling, expiry and current quote. Model review records retain actual requested/reported identity, material findings, evidence and dispositions. Review may be discharged by the delegated analytical workflow; it does not require the absent user to approve each assertion.

## Initial paper capital-budget choice

Adopt three £100 maximum per-idea budgets for the first paper cohort, at most one new unreported instruction, and no more than £100 exposed to one issuer or shared catalyst. This is a transparent experimental choice, not an optimal allocation derived from the user's loss tolerance. Compare two £150 and four £75 variants without selecting the best historical result as if prospectively chosen. Initial per-idea budgets remain fixed; profits stay in sleeve cash pending cohort review. A budget is a ceiling, not an instruction to invest it.

No numerical stop or return probability is invented. Each accepted opportunity supplies economically reasoned adverse prices, reassessment conditions and a priced-loss alternative before entry. The paired comparison holds entry selection constant: one arm uses that frozen price-loss condition with a documented feasible order; another uses frozen thesis deterioration/time exits. Both permit common profit-taking only if specified before entry. Missing thresholds mean no accepted entry in that comparison. Early-exit proceeds stay in cash until the common deadline.

## Implemented paper records

`tools/paper.py` submits structured named operations to the separate `state/paper` database. Protocols are frozen and hashed; amendments create new cohorts. Record accepted, rejected and missed opportunities contemporaneously, executable quotes, response delay, partial entries/exits and real supplied fees/FX. No touched limit implies a fill. Missing marks or benchmark inputs remain unknown. These records neither mutate the live sleeve nor send instructions.

Freshness and delay must be supplied for each cohort with a rationale matched to its events and human response assumptions. Test hours and days of delay; reject opportunities whose mechanism cannot tolerate them. The 15-minute refreshed-confirmation ceiling remains a communication safeguard bounded by earlier price, event, evidence and expiry deadlines.

## Remaining acceptance work

The artifact verifier, atomic pre-instruction reservations, persistent decision lane and connected paper pipeline are implemented and tested with fictional fixtures. This verifies mechanics, not an actual opportunity. A real JPM dossier and dated pre-event rejection are saved; independent review findings remain separate from the immutable original. Complete required analytical challenge, retain the rejection, and research another candidate if the evidence remains inadequate. Exercise an accepted paper path only when contemporaneous evidence supports it; do not invent an entry to demonstrate the software. Verify phone delivery, actual broker reconciliation and execution-data coverage. The live observation coordinator remains nonactionable while these dependencies are incomplete.
