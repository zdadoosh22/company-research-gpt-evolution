The configured disclosure passes remain 09:00 and 18:00 London. A Netflix or AMD release after 18:00 would ordinarily precede the next scheduled 09:00 pass. SEC filing arrival can lag the issuer release, retrieval can fail, and the computer can be unavailable. For Uber, the same-day 18:00 pass follows the call. None of these passes guarantees complete event capture, prompt review or immediate phone delivery. Existing feed coverage is limited to configured sources.

Before a report, preserve the most recent management baseline and comparable external expectations, including definitions and publication cutoffs. After it, first authenticate and read the complete package; separate reported actuals, guidance revisions, accounting scope, cash timing and management interpretation. Compare material changes with the frozen baseline, then reassess persistent per-share economics and price requirements. A price gap, earnings beat or news headline never automatically creates an entry.

For a delayed response, reassess the opportunity at the then-current evidence and price. Retain correct-but-late, no-repricing and adverse outcomes rather than pretending execution happened at the announcement. If the opportunity has passed, record it as unavailable. Phone status/expiry and portfolio reservations belong to the separate instruction pipeline once its gates pass.
