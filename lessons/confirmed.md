# Confirmed research lessons

- Business quality, present valuation and prospective return answer different questions. A discount to present value does not by itself establish attractive forward returns.
- Cost basis is historical context and must not change intrinsic value or future business assumptions.
- Source-period, unit, entity and accounting disagreements require evidence, not plausible AI explanations.
- Numerical templates do not prove that a forecast is defensible. Valuation assumptions must follow business evidence.
- A dated file archive can be reloaded by future chats; conversational memory is not a durable research database.

These principles are approved by the project plan. Historical claims of three complete APP revision/test cycles were not independently verified and are not evidence of validation.

## Focused pilot audit, 7 October 2026

- A borrowing receipt requires linked future debt service and an outstanding claim schedule. A cash-only input creates fictitious equity value. The starter pattern now rejects nonzero net borrowing.
- Terminal operating cash and fixed financing claims cannot both be mechanically grown. Constant interest is valued separately from growing operating cash in the corrected illustrative pattern.
- Every sensitivity setting must satisfy its own economic domains; a valid active case does not validate all grid cells.
- Missing inputs must protect supporting ratios, sensitivities and headline outputs. Invalid case selectors must be checked before selection, including pasted fractional or text values.
- Rounded principal, carrying debt, GAAP financing expense and cash interest are different observations. Record exact definitions and snapshot dates before constructing cash flows.
- Weighted EPS awards are not an exact outstanding claim schedule. Known historical grants narrow the evidence gap but do not verify a current roll-forward.
- A bank return measured against average tangible equity is not automatically a forecast return on opening tangible equity. A capital-ratio comparison must preserve the issuer's stated scope.
- An XIRR solver needs both a valid bracket and a residual check; a near-total loss can fall below an arbitrary numerical search bound.
- Deterministic formula checks and stronger-model review exposed material errors. Neither establishes that an illustrative company forecast is defensible. The corrected pilots remain partial.
