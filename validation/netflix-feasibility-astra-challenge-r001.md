## A limited construction showing what can be tested

This is an independent reviewer diagnostic, **not a supported sustainable-cash floor, a valuation or a complete forecast**. It shows a workable interface for conservative ranges and exposes the assumptions to challenge rather than concealing them behind an unallocated receipt tax.

Use the low end of the July revenue guide times its operating margin: $16,065m operating income. Conditional 2026 amortisation is $16,422.166m times 1.10 = $18,064.3826m. Then explicitly assume $800m recurring cash interest, no recurring nonoperating income, prospective recurring cash tax of 30% of operating income less interest, a 1.16 cash-content/amortisation ratio, and $2,000m for all remaining net cash drag after noncontent depreciation. The resulting pre-principal equity-cash diagnostic is:

`(16,065 − 800) × (1 − 0.30) − 18,064.3826 × (1.16 − 1) − 2,000 = 5,795.198784 million`.

If those economics persisted into a later stress year, adding a hypothetical full $4bn uncovered payment after June 2027, with no tax relief, replacement spending or extra revenue credited, would leave $1,795.198784m **before mandatory principal**. It does not allocate $4bn to FY2026, repeat it annually or assert that $4bn is a contractual maximum. Complete forecast coverage instead leaves the extra subtraction at zero; partial coverage subtracts only the uncovered amount at specified dates.

These assumptions have different evidential strength. The OI and amortisation start from dated guidance; 1.16 is near the observed H1 cash-content ratio of 1.1620, deliberately treating a potentially seasonal high ratio as a stress rather than an established steady state. $800m interest is above FY2025 reported interest expense of $776.510m, which itself included about $60m transaction-financing cost; subsequent debt/coupons still require a schedule. A 30% prospective tax burden is a conservative stress relative to the cited 2025 cash ratio and 21% US federal statutory reference, not a legal upper bound or forecast. The $2bn remaining drag is **an unvalidated analyst stress parameter that must be decomposed**; it must not become a hidden plug or be called evidence-backed merely because this equation produces positive cash. GAAP OI retains SBC expense, so this version does not subtract SBC again. Other noncash items, leases, working capital, acquisition reinvestment and tax timing must be explicitly reconciled to that residual.

Thus this example proves neither persistence nor fair value. It does demonstrate that neither unallocated receipt tax nor the disclosed unknown-title range logically forces the combined cash model to be absent. Varying these assumptions and testing their persistence is the authorised analytical work. The cash result cannot be converted into an equity value without a debt/funding/distribution path and a justified capitalisation rule.

## Concrete completion and review conditions

1. **Build causally distinct paths from the public aggregate economics.** H1 revenue rose $3,187.728m and OI $1,027.914m, an observed incremental conversion of 32.2460%. This is an historical aggregate response, not a marginal forecast guarantee. Use it, earlier annual changes and the FY26 guide to justify a range; test slower growth or revenue stagnation with resilient content costs, guide-like economics, and stronger monetisation that pays for its required spend. The externally displayed FY27 revenue growth of about 11.18% is a comparison point, not the engine. No private cohort data is necessary. Cases cannot assume an unchanged margin after a demand shock without explaining variable/fixed costs.
2. **Reconcile recurring cash in at least one forecast year and its fade.** Separate amortisation and content cash, prospective taxes, cash interest, SBC treatment, noncontent capital expenditure/depreciation, working capital and any recurring acquisitions. Use historical cash conversion as a cross-check: FY2025 FCF less SBC was $9,092.604m, before normalisation, not a sustainable floor. Explicitly reconcile tax cash versus accrued tax; do not import the total effective rate to allocate the termination fee. Broader defensible ranges are preferable to false precision.
3. **Calculate the commitment cases, including timing.** Include coverage, partial overlap and adverse uncovered schedules; make the latter mostly later than June 2027 consistent with the reviewed disclosure. Explain tax/replacement treatment, and separately test an amount beyond management's range if used as an adverse robustness check. Never add known commitments or recognised liabilities on top of spending that already pays them.
4. **Use one coherent valuation basis and a meaningful cross-check.** An operating FCFF build with operating taxes/WACC and a dated claims bridge is available; so is a genuinely funded FCFE build with equity rates. Do not feed reported levered FCF into WACC, deduct debt twice, create free refinancing cash or assume all June cash is surplus. Known-award gross dilution can be a conservative dated sensitivity. Existing unvested awards and future cash-equivalent compensation must not count the same compensation twice. Preserve the valuation date: all FY2026 cash cannot be discounted as future cash from October. A conditional January 2027 framework is acceptable if explicitly labelled and if its historical claims/cash basis is bridged or treated as a stated pro forma condition; it is not October intrinsic value or an entry ceiling.
5. **Justify rates and terminal economics independently of the price.** The 9.49% market reference is a starting comparison, not Netflix's rate. A judgemental required-return range can be justified through competitive concentration, operating leverage/content rigidity, geographic/currency risk and financing; exact beta is not required. If a broad 10–14% range is explored, explicitly defend the additional risk premium and show sensitivity rather than presenting endpoints as measured facts. Zero/low mature growth and a funded fade are legitimate conservative cases; positive terminal growth needs corresponding reinvestment. An earnings cross-check must reconcile cash conversion and duration, not reuse an unsupported chosen P/E or mechanically average methods.
6. **Keep expectations and the short horizon separate.** Management/model-implied requirements can be shown without quarterly vendor consensus. Repeat no-repricing, compression, adverse-surprise, delayed recognition and hour/day response cases against the constructed economics. A fair-value range or positive owner cash does not establish an unexpected improvement, price response or superiority to cash over days to months.

Acceptance is a transparent conditional framework with economically explained assumptions and inspectable results, not a narrow fair-value interval or an approved entry. If modest, plausible changes dominate the result, report that fragility and withhold the dependent conclusion. Rechallenge new numerical economics independently before parent disposition.
