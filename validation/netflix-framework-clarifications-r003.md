# Netflix framework wording clarification, r003

10 October2026London. This is a correction to prose only; all frozen r001/r002 files and numeric economics are unchanged.

In frameworkr002 Results, “negative annual distribution” is inaccurate. A negative **FCFE** is a funding use; the model permits **zero available distribution**, with cash drawn from the explicitly conditional opening reserve. It never pays a negative distribution.

In the downside with the **4bn full disclosed-range-endpoint stress**, all yearly FCFE stay positive; the smallest is2028 **188.088544 million USD**. Minimum modeled reserve remains9,127.910million. In the **6bn beyond-management-range stress**,2028 FCFE is **−811.911456 million USD**; distribution is zero and the reserve falls to **8,315.998544 million USD**. Figures here are rounded to six decimal places from the saved model. These are conditional modeled flows, not actual cash statements, legal bounds or investment returns.

This clarification supersedes that results paragraph only. It does not change cash schedules, component values, funding assumptions, approval gates or valuation methodology. The full4bn uncovered case alone does not create a reserve draw in the actual saved model. The6bn robustness case does; it remains annually funded under the named pro forma condition, with intrayear/current liquidity unverified.
