Corrected accounting shell (nine months ended 30 August 2026)

Main identity. I retreat to the identity:

CFO_cont = 585.2 − CFO_disc, where CFO_disc is UNKNOWN.

Accrual decomposition, with no overlapping terms:

CFO_disc = (−8.8) + (−33.6) + R_disc = −42.4 + R_disc

−8.8 is Dockers' net loss, sitting inside consolidated net income.
−33.6 is the gain adjustment in the indirect method.
R_disc is one combined, unquantified term covering every other Dockers reconciling item:
non-cash income items other than the gain;
deferred tax;
changes in all operating assets and liabilities, including income tax payable/receivable and accrued disposal costs.

Tax and cost-accrual movements sit only inside R_disc. They are not counted again anywhere else.

What −42.4 is. It is an accrual shell only (= −26.7 − 16.3 + 0.6). It is not Dockers' cash, not an estimate of continuing CFO, and not normalised owner cash. Note 2 puts no numerical bound on R_disc. So no number or direction for CFO_cont follows from it.

Recurring owner cash. It cannot be derived. Even after the 57.1 refund (verified as CFO in the separately inspected MD&A) is removed, 528.1 is still a consolidated sensitivity that strips out principal only. It is not a continuing figure. Still unknown:

CFO_disc;

capex attributable to Dockers.

ASR. The January 200m buyback falls within the nine months but before Q3. It is out of CFO because it is classified as financing, not because of when it was paid.
