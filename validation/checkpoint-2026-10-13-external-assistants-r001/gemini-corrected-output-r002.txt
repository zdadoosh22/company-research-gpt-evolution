4. Scope Disconnect: E-Commerce Growth vs. Store Comps

The prior inference that +10% e-commerce growth mathematically forced brick-and-mortar store comps to be negative is withdrawn.

Total e-commerce (+10% reported/organic) measures total digital revenue (including new digital market entries, app expansions, and concessions), whereas DTC comparable sales (+0.4%) measure only mature omni-channel locations.

Hypothetical Counterexample:

Assume DTC comp sales are split 20% Comp Digital and 80% Comp Stores.

If the total 10% e-commerce growth is driven primarily by new non-comp digital expansion, while Comp Digital grew only +0.5%, then Comp Stores grew +0.375%:

(0.20×0.5%)+(0.80×0.375%)=0.1%+0.3%=0.4% blended comp

Because non-comp digital revenue is excluded from comp calculations, positive overall e-commerce does not require negative store comps.
