## A limited construction showing what can be tested

This is an independent reviewer diagnostic, **not a supported sustainable-cash floor, a valuation or a complete forecast**. It shows a workable interface for conservative ranges and exposes the assumptions to challenge rather than concealing them behind an unallocated receipt tax.

The resulting pre-principal equity-cash diagnostic is:

`(16,065 − 800) × (1 − 0.30) − 18,064.3826 × (1.16 − 1) − 2,000 = 5,795.198784 million`.

If those economics persisted into a later stress year, adding a hypothetical full $4bn uncovered payment after June 2027, with no tax relief, replacement spending or extra revenue credited, would leave $1,795.198784m **before mandatory principal**. Complete forecast coverage instead leaves the extra subtraction at zero; partial coverage subtracts only the uncovered amount at specified dates.
