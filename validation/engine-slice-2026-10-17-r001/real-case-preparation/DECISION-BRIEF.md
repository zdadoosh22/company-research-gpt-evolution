- **Resolve:** distinguish what the announcement actually commits to from
  examples, partner endorsements and forward-looking intentions. Check whether
  it supplies quantified orders, realized economics or a defined realization
  timetable before claiming an earnings/cash revision.
- **Bound:** evaluate the proposed event-to-business mechanism qualitatively
  where justified. No invented probability, adoption rate, margin or financial
  forecast. Explicitly distinguish absence in this packet from absence everywhere.
- **Park:** any claimed capital-risking opportunity that lacks a supportable
  expectations, durable per-claim economics and delay-resistant price-response
  chain. Do not buy merely because the product could be useful.
