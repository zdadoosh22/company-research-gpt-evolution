## The favourable mechanism and strongest opposition

The prospective economic case is that price increases retain members, advertisements add contribution rather than merely replacing subscription receipts, and these receipts grow faster than recurring content and other costs. An October disclosure could make a larger future cash stream more credible. A durable change can matter after hours or days of manual delay if its value exceeds the price available then. A release date, rising viewing time or better reported EPS alone supplies none of that inequality.

The strongest opposing case is that the apparent inflection is already in guidance and partly a comparison effect, while the cash cost of content is rising faster than its amortized expense. A business can achieve reported profit growth and still fail to produce unexpectedly higher recurring distributable cash. The following new comparator test makes this objection concrete.

## Q3's five-point margin gain largely laps a prior operating tax charge

The issuer's [Q3 2025 letter](https://s22.q4cdn.com/959853165/files/doc_financials/2025/q3/FINAL-Q3-25-Shareholder-Letter.pdf), pp.1-2 and 11, reports Q3 operating income $3,248.247m and revenue $11,510.307m. It explains an **approximately $619m Brazilian non-income tax expense**, covering 2022 through Q3 2025, booked in cost of revenue; about 20% related to 2025. This is an operating expense, distinct from income tax on the 2026 termination receipt. The [2025 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1065280/000106528025000406/nflx-20250930.htm), Note 8 and MD&A cost of revenue, corroborates it through web reading.

Removing that entire approximate charge solely as a comparator sensitivity gives ($3,248.247m + $619m) / $11,510.307m = **33.598%** Q3 2025 margin. July's Q3 2026 guide is $4,268m operating profit on $12,860m revenue, or **33.188%** from rounded inputs (issuer displays 33.2%). Thus the guide is about **0.410 percentage points lower** than the adjusted comparator, and operating-profit growth is **10.363%**, versus **31.394%** against precise reported 2025 profit. The original screen's 31.404% uses rounded $3,248m; both are valid reported-basis arithmetic, neither proves persistent underlying acceleration.

This sensitivity does not declare Brazilian tax costs permanently absent, rewrite GAAP, erase potential deposits or settle the dispute. Management's expectation of little future effect is an interested claim. Because part of the charge relates to the current year, removing all $619m is deliberately an upper adjustment, not our normalized operating forecast. It nevertheless defeats attributing the entire five-point comparison to new pricing/advertising efficiency.

The existing FY guide also already implies Q4 margin approximately 27.36%-27.48%. Q2's small margin beat reflects expense timing. The decisive October evidence is therefore the joint change in Q3 actuals, FY/Q4 and the supportable future run rate, after separating Brazil, content timing, FX and operating costs. Sources: [Q2 2026 issuer letter](https://s22.q4cdn.com/959853165/files/doc_financials/2026/q2/FINAL-Q2-26-Shareholder-Letter.pdf), pp.1-2,9,14; prior discovery screen's reproducible Q4 bridge.

## Cash normalization is a bridge with missing taxes, not a subtraction verdict

H1 reported FCF $6,619.243m includes the $2,800m termination cash receipt; it is below operating income and must not be removed from operating margin. Let T_cash be **actual H1 cash income taxes attributable to that receipt**. Removing only the receipt and its related tax cash would yield $3,819.243m + T_cash. T_cash is undisclosed/unquantified in the reviewed sources; the $3,819.243m subtraction retains those tax outflows and is not after-tax normalized FCF. Similarly the ~$12,500m FY FCF guide minus $2,800m is $9,700m + unknown related FY cash taxes before other recurring adjustments. Neither formula is a recurring cash forecast. Net-income normalization needs the receipt's associated income-tax provision, distinct from cash taxes; the H1 effective tax rate cannot be substituted mechanically. Sources: Q2 letter pp.6,9,11-12; [June 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1065280/000106528026000212/nflx-20260630.htm), Note 6 and MD&A cash flows.

Content payments inferred from cash-flow additions and liability movement are $9,774.440m + $136.578m = **$9,911.018m**, compared with $7,385.470m + $625.305m = **$8,010.775m** in H1 2025. Those amounts are 1.1620x and 1.0465x content amortization. Cash payments grew **23.72%**, amortization **11.42%**; payments exceeded amortization by $1,381.809m versus $355.589m. This is a material cash timing/reinvestment burden, not proof of worse economics: title delivery, release timing and liability settlements can shift between halves. Q2 letter p.11 supplies all inputs.

Management's ~1.1x FY cash-content/amortization guide imposes a useful test. If A is full-year amortization, H2 cash content must be approximately 1.1A - $9,911.018m, while H2 amortization is A - $8,529.209m; H2 excess cash spend is therefore approximately 0.1A - $1,381.809m. Do not annualize H1's ratio or capitalize the amortization addback without replacement spending. A justified annual A and spending/liability schedule are still needed.

H1 SBC's $271.717m cash-flow addback is not costless owner income. Address it through a consistent claims/dilution schedule or cash-equivalent cost treatment, once. H1 buybacks $5,984.991m use cash; acquisition spending $585.744m is outside the issuer CFO-minus-capex FCF definition. These are explicit capital uses, not automatically permanent recurring deductions. Existing obligations and discretionary repurchases must fit funding before claiming distributable surplus. ## Conditional valuation and response-price requirements

For a coherent equity earnings method, let E be supportable forward earnings per split-adjusted share after sustainable taxes and ownership effects, and M a separately justified capitalization multiple. Value = M x E. At the dated $71.57 reference, the required E is $71.57/M; this algebra identifies the question without choosing a return hurdle or reverse-fitting M. Operating cash valuation instead requires cash content, cash taxes, capex, working capital, SBC/claims and the debt/interest bridge. No numerical M, discount rate, terminal growth, scenario probabilities or target is invented.

A durable revision must survive the response-price test. If earnings rise from E0 to E1 and the multiple changes from M0 to M1, price support changes in proportion to (E1/E0) x (M1/M0); even correct earnings improvement can be offset by multiple compression. A temporary quarter's cost deferral changes the timing of earnings, not necessarily the recurring E. For a modest days-to-months window, expected revised cash flows can change current value immediately, but a five-year end value cannot be relabelled a near-term target.
