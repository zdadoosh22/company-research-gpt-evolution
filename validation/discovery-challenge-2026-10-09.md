# Independent discovery challenge: AMD, Uber and Netflix

9 October 2026. **Completed substantive independent screening review; no accepted entry.** Research priority is **Netflix first, AMD second, Uber third**. This ranks the usefulness and tractability of the next research step, not expected shareholder returns or current-price attractiveness. Missing quotes or execution readiness do not establish that any company is overvalued or that cash will outperform.

The selected review route was **gpt-6-astra, high reasoning**, explicitly confirmed by parent orchestration. The reviewer was a separate agent with a fresh task context. Backend identity was not independently exposed or verified. This record documents actual analytical work and its findings; it is not a backend-model certificate or a full thesis. The approved methodology remains v1.0.0; no base methodology, original study or operational state was changed.

## Disposition and research priority

**1. Netflix: advance to deeper scoped opportunity research now.** Nearest scheduled report, explicit FY/Q3 baseline and an inspectable Q4 margin/content-cash bridge make the persistence question comparatively testable. This is information-value prioritization, not a priced-return ranking.

**2. Amd: retain and deepen focused demand-margin-funding work.** Large possible persistent earnings revisions are testable against unusually useful September primary guidance, but customer-linked financing, ramp execution and conditional ownership create more interacting assumptions.

**3. Uber: retain conditional candidate; resolve scope and financing before a positive dossier.** Operating improvement is credible, but major transaction value realization is later and the packet misses September commentary, a completed financing roll-forward and Q3 consolidation effects.

The strongest challenge to this ordering is that AMD may offer more economically consequential new information and already has better archived management updates. Netflix remains first because its nearer report and explicit annual bridge permit a narrower, falsifiable persistence test. This judgement must change if fresh evidence makes that test unanswerable or materially strengthens another candidate. There is no numerical probability or return ranking.

### N1: Separate termination cash from operating margin and recurring cash

**high for positive entry; known gap plus quantified challenge.** The $2.8bn receipt is recorded below operating income. It therefore does not contaminate the H1 operating-income figure used in the Q4 margin bridge. It does materially contaminate net income and FCF: gross receipt is 42.30% of H1 FCF. FCF less gross receipt is $3,819.243m, but this retains related tax cash outflows and is NOT after-tax normalized cash. Content cash payments derived from additions plus the liability reduction are $9,911.018m, or 1.1620x amortization, versus 1.0465x in prior H1. Management guides about 1.1x for the full year. Timing and future spending require a full-year bridge.

**Required treatment:** Normalize receipt and actual related taxes once; reconcile cash content, amortization and content liabilities. Keep operating-income guidance intact unless a separate operating adjustment is supported; do not subtract the receipt from operating income.

**Disposition:** Expanded here; after-tax normalized cash remains unresolved.

**Evidence:** Q2 issuer PDF pp.6,9,11-12; 10-Q mirror Note 6 (issuer link unproved).

### N2: Q4 implied margin is a baseline, not a promised improvement or deterioration

**medium; expectations challenge.** The original Q4 implied revenue, operating-income and margin calculations all reproduce. The ~27.4% Q4 margins already accommodate a fall from Q3 33.2%. Q2 beat partly reflects expense timing; Q3 prior-year comparison and content seasonality must be understood before attributing all ~31.4% guided profit growth to durable pricing/ads economics. Ad revenue guide is ~5.86% of group revenue, not its incremental profit contribution.

**Required treatment:** Compare Q3 actual plus revised FY/Q4 and 2027 expectations against matched definitions, dated external estimates and eventual price requirements. Quantify ad contribution and retention economics only from supportable inputs.

**Disposition:** Original bridge accepted; surprise/persistence unproved.

**Evidence:** Q2 issuer PDF pp.1-2,9,14.

### N3: Preserve filing-mirror and July-financing qualifications and split units

**high for positive entry; source and funding limitation.** Issuer PDF is archived and hash-matched; filing mirror issuer linkage is still unproved. July filing confirms a $1bn 5.25% 2036 issuance and intended repayment of 2026 notes, not proof that repayment occurred. Reviewer re-read the SEC web document but has not created an original local archive. June cash, carrying debt, principal, future interest and content commitments cannot be merged into one current net-debt figure. Ten-for-one adjusted shares and EPS remain mandatory.

**Required treatment:** Archive/authenticate material original filings and roll financing forward. Maintain separate cash/debt/content/lease schedules, avoid double deduction of operating content spend, and verify any future price series on the same split basis.

**Disposition:** Qualifications retained; no positive-entry source approval.

**Evidence:** Q2 issuer PDF pp.9-11,14; original JSON source register; July 22 SEC 8-K Item 8.01 re-opened during review.

### A1: The lower provider EPS estimate is not a defensible surprise hurdle

**high for positive entry; expectations challenge.** Recomputed guide-implied adjusted EPS $1.931295 is essentially the $1.94 display, not the unresolved-basis $1.35 estimate. September material already communicates a substantial 2027 ramp and lower gross-margin percentage during MI450 ramp. A 100bp margin loss offsets about $232.14m of revenue at 56% gross margin before any extra opex. Unit availability, systems execution, mix and operating leverage must move together.

**Required treatment:** Use updated comparable estimates and September management baseline. Do not average providers, assume a universal CPU-per-GW multiplier, or equate high revenue growth with an unanticipated per-share gain.

**Disposition:** Original bridge accepted; positive surprise case unresolved.

**Evidence:** August 4 issuer call PDF pp.6-7; TipRanks and AltIndex archived estimates; Citi September 8 pp.2-4,6-7; Goldman September 11 pp.4-6,8-9.

### A2: Quantify guarantees and leases omitted from the short cash summary

**high for positive entry; material expansion.** Original screen flags leases/guarantees but omits their June magnitudes: $4.1bn maximum gross partner-lease guarantees and $4.5bn uncommenced leases expected to begin H2 2026. These differ from the separate subsequent $9.5bn leases starting 2027/28 and $30.276bn unconditional commitments, including $17.386bn remainder-2026. Guarantees may correlate adversely with customer cash collections and investment exposure. Gross maxima are not immediately payable debt. The $2.170bn payables inflow sensitivity does not measure normalized FCF or subtract all other working-capital effects.

**Required treatment:** Create dated payment, guarantee-trigger and overlap schedules, include capex accrued but unpaid ($421m), and refresh the September $875m note maturity. Challenge customer financing together with demand, not as independent risks.

**Disposition:** Expanded here; full holding-window funding bridge remains open.

**Evidence:** June 10-Q Note 8 Lease Guarantees; Note 9; Note 10 Commitments; H1 cash-flow statement.

### A3: Warrant and World Labs dilution must be causally linked to business outcomes

**high for positive entry; ownership challenge.** Maximum 320m warrants equal 19.61% of rounded June actual shares; existing holders retain 83.61% after hypothetical full exercise. This is a 16.39% ownership reduction, not 19.61% off every per-share value, and is not current dilution. Maximum exercise brings only $3.2m proceeds. Vesting requires purchase, price/performance and further commercial conditions. World Labs share issue depends on ten-trading-day VWAP ending two trading days before close; add shares and acquired economics together.

**Required treatment:** Keep EPS weighted shares distinct from valuation-date claims; model milestones, proceeds and purchase/acquisition value consistently. Do not deduct full warrant fair value and also fully dilute for the same claim without a coherent settlement treatment.

**Disposition:** Original conditional stress accepted; no complete claim valuation.

**Evidence:** June 10-Q Note 12 Warrants; September 28 8-K Item 3.02.

### U1: Tender acceptance cannot bring forward 2027 acquisition earnings

**high for positive entry; timing challenge.** November 5 acceptance expiry is 24:00 Frankfurt at end of day, with settlement expected H2 2027 subject to conditions. Delivery Hero accretion at close/year three is outside the immediate earnings event. It can affect present financing/risk expectations, but is not November earned profit. The archived notice is not the complete legal offer. ezCater has cash consideration and conditional coming-months timing; its headline bookings do not establish absolute incremental owner profit.

**Required treatment:** Separate acceptance, regulatory conditions, closing, consolidation and synergies. Build transaction cash/financing schedules before incorporating benefits; preserve no immediate accretion premise.

**Disposition:** Original rejection reasoning upheld; transaction economics open.

**Evidence:** July 16 acquisition-offer release; August 27 offer-publication release lines 36,39-44; October 6 ezCater release.

### U2: Insurance and SBC sensitivities need persistence and scope reconciliation

**high for positive entry; accounting challenge.** Q2/H1 FCF, CFO SBC sensitivities and insurance contributions reproduce. Non-GAAP operating income/EPS retain SBC; EBITDA excludes it. $556m/$1,029m expense versus $550m/$1,023m CFO addback versus $549m/$1,022m EBITDA adjustment remains unreconciled. Insurance accrual inflows are timing effects already linked to recognized expense; subtracting all of them permanently could understate a growing steady-state business, while treating them as unrestricted recurring owner cash could overstate it. Combined H1 sensitivity $3,225m is NOT normalized FCF.

**Required treatment:** Reconcile compensation scopes before the next profit model; model settlement/reserve-development and collateral, not blanket permanent deductions. Treat buybacks and dilution once and connect them to affordability.

**Disposition:** Known gap retained; no invented explanation or normalized owner-cash claim.

**Evidence:** Q2 issuer release cash flows and definitions lines 851-919; SEC Note 7 rendered excerpt.

### U3: The Q3 organic bridge must include Careem control and expiring funding availability

**high for positive entry; specific omitted roll-forward items.** The saved 10-Q excerpt states Careem Technologies control was obtained July 30 and its impact was being evaluated. A Q3 growth comparison therefore needs consolidation/organic separation beyond Delivery Hero and ezCater. The June term-loan facility had $2bn drawn and $1bn unused that expired August 2 if undrawn; that unused amount cannot be carried forward as October liquidity. Existing Delivery Hero influence/equity-method accounting is also different from later control.

**Required treatment:** Read subsequent acquisition/financing disclosures and September management webcast, reconcile organic growth and funding, and obtain complete primary archives where current packet has only excerpts.

**Disposition:** Added precise research requirements; no claim current financing failed.

**Evidence:** Archived SEC rendered extract sec-vat-financing-cfo: Note 3 lines 708-709; Note 5 lines 788-790; Note 3 Delivery Hero lines 695-696.
