## Calculations and substantive challenge

**F1 — Information; accepted within limits.** Public-price proceeds are $4,744.1875m, derived separately from face and the four issue prices. Underwriting totals $8.1m; proceeds before other expenses are $4,736.0875m. Subtracting estimated $9.7m other expenses gives $4,726.3875m estimated net. Face less that estimate is $23.6125m, combining original-issue discount, underwriting and estimated costs. It is neither final paid expense nor an isolated equity-value loss. The actual closing establishes borrowing, not final use of proceeds.

New annual contractual coupons independently sum to $57.5m + $75m + $52.5m + $55m = $240m. A regular half-year is $120m. Interest starts August17,2026; the first nominal payment is February17,2027; the source uses twelve 30-day months and moves non-business-day payments to the next business day. This is new-note service, not total issuer interest or an observed future payment.

S18 is a June snapshot adjusted only for issuance. $8,000m rounded principal less $48m rounded carrying adjustments gives $7,952m; the retained $875m row disproves a repayment assumption in that table. The footnote preserves approximately $0.065m convertible principal. The note correctly flags it instead of treating a table dash as zero.

$5,086m cash + $8,025m short-term investments + $4,726.3875m estimated net gives $17,837.3875m, excluding $11m restricted cash and holding all other flows constant. Hypothetical repayment of $875m yields $16,962.3875m assets and $7,125m principal. The simplified difference remains $9,837.3875m in both cases, excluding the tiny residual. This is an identity, not present excess cash or an equity valuation. The study labels it accordingly.

**F2 — Low; downstream requirement, not a verified error.** A real roll-forward must include old coupons too. If outstanding for the normal period, September24 service comprises $18.4275m on the $875m 4.212% tranche and $13.496875m on the $625m 4.319% tranche. A maturity date proves neither repayment nor default. The present note explicitly excludes interest from its principal-only illustration, so no correction is required to that example. Downstream work must verify settlement and use an actual dated debt-service schedule.

**F3 — Information; obligations treatment accepted, mapping incomplete.** The six commitment buckets sum to $30,276m. $17,386m is June's remainder-of-year obligation schedule, not October unpaid obligations. Supplier inputs, cloud capacity and licences normally enter operating cash or working capital; subtracting them wholesale after a forecast of those cash uses would double count. Conversely, anticipated assignment or cancellation does not demonstrate economically free exit or that every exposure is already captured.

The $4,100m maximum guarantees are contingent gross exposure, not drawn loans. Commenced leases near $1,200m, uncommenced $4,500m, subsequent $9,500m leases and conditional investments up to $5,000m differ in period and trigger. A present-value lease liability is also not the same measurement as lifetime undiscounted rent. The paper properly refuses both an indiscriminate debt pile and an unsupported claim of ample current headroom. Contract/counterparty overlap remains a required investigation, not a presumed offset.

Cash flow checks yield $5,321m minus $1,197m = $4,124m. Removing the payables inflow alone gives $1,954m. All listed working-capital changes together equal negative $274m; therefore the payable sensitivity is deliberately one-sided and cannot estimate normalized owner cash. Its existing label is appropriate. The $421m accrued unpaid capex observation must be mapped to eventual capex cash and payables, not deducted twice. The $100m related-party loan is not collected cash merely because October is its maturity month.

**F4 — Low; downstream requirement, not a verified error.** Cash plus short-term investments is a useful dated aggregate, but a payment schedule needs the separate maturity/sale/settlement assumptions and prevailing value of investments. Note8 includes debt securities of longer contractual maturity in the overall investment portfolio. No arbitrary haircut is justified by this review. The restricted long-term investments correctly remain outside the illustration. Programme capacity and an undrawn revolver are not cash or assured additive $10.5bn capacity.

**F5 — Information; conditional ownership logic accepted.** Two maximum 160m warrants create 320m shares for only $3.2m exercise cash. On July29 common shares of 1,632.475042m, full hypothetical exercise yields 1,952.475042m shares, 83.6105459% old-owner retention, 19.6021374% share addition and 16.3894541% ownership reduction. The original June-rounded diagnostic remains separate. Neither period-average diluted EPS shares nor June unvested warrants establishes today's claims.

For unchanged total distributable value, old ownership is N/(N+W+S); maintaining per-share economics requires proportionate additional distributable value after consistently accounting for proceeds and acquired claims. That is algebra, not a claim that dilution necessarily destroys value. High demand may support cash while earning warrants; weak demand may avoid vesting and strand fixed commitments. Meta's supplier diversification challenges exclusivity assumptions. The record correctly couples both directions.

World Labs uses future contractual pricing, not today's market quote. Before customary adjustments, 8,200 divided by that USD-per-share pricing basis gives shares in millions. The actual agreement's averaging convention governs; this shorthand does not establish a numeric share forecast. All-stock consideration avoids an $8.2bn cash purchase subtraction but does not imply free acquired assets, profits or accretion. The source's conditional close is not a completed transaction.
