# Acceptance standard

## Every material numerical result
- Trace source value, publication date, reporting period, units, currency, entity/scope and ownership basis.
- Reconcile TTM, restatements and accounting adjustments. Explain missing values and incompatible definitions.
- Independently check valuation arithmetic, claims bridge, terminal assumptions, timing, dividends and dilution.
- Reconcile current and horizon values from the same forecast and stated future financing/distributions.
- Compare method-appropriate cross-checks; investigate rather than mechanically average disagreements.

## Every deliverable
- Report/summary/workbook use the same reviewed result snapshot.
- Summary at most five readable pages; all PDF pages visually inspected for clipping and readability.
- Workbook preserves formulas, source actuals and separately editable What-if. Test input changes, active cases, blanks versus valid zeros, sensitivity base match and export round-trip. Identify native Excel behaviour not tested.
- No unexpected formula errors or unresolved material arithmetic errors. Deliberate missing-data markers are visible.
- Claims and conclusions reflect opposing evidence, forecast uncertainty and modelled returns.
- Run record includes source coverage, assumptions, failures, review findings and precise status.
- Prior publication hashes remain unchanged during updates. Fresh-chat instructions resolve the correct current methodology and company history.

## Pilot programme
APP full thesis/package; JPM focused bank model and capital/return checks; Unilever focused IFRS, period, security/currency and provenance checks; controlled missing-data, finite-runway, contradictory-source and ambiguous-security fixtures. Stronger Astra review is separate from deterministic and self-review checks. Record actual review identity; never fabricate it.

Global research coverage is intended, but untested sector templates remain provisional. Numerical conclusions may be withheld even when the qualitative research is finished.
