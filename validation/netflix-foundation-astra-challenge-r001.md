## Verified defects and corrections

### NFLX-ASTRA-001 — Low: the machine-readable cash field names the wrong cash population

`netflix-foundation-calculations-r001.py` and its generated JSON store `cash.restricted_cash_change = 63.592`. The June 10-Q cash-flow statement p.5 identifies 63.592 as the change in **cash, cash equivalents and restricted cash combined**, in USD millions. Restricted cash alone falls from 5.513 to 3.554, a change of -1.959; unrestricted cash rises 65.551. These three figures reconcile exactly. The foundation prose and `restricted_component_change` already describe the distinction correctly.

This is a verified semantic data defect, not a failed arithmetic identity. A downstream consumer could select the misleading field and report the wrong restricted-cash movement. In a new revision, rename that field to `cash_cash_equivalents_and_restricted_change` or an equally explicit combined name; retain the -1.959 restricted-only field. Verify that the renamed output still totals 63.592 = 65.551 - 1.959 and that no numerical inputs change. Outcome here: open for parent correction; no change to withholding or the June liquidity bridge.

### NFLX-ASTRA-002 — Low: the calculation caution assigns audit status to an unaudited letter table

The same PY/JSON `content.guidance_caution` calls the 2025 amortization input “audited annual amortization from Q4 letter p14.” The original Q4 letter p.14 visibly labels its financial table **unaudited**. The separately archived 2025 10-K cash-flow statement and Note 5 corroborate 16,422.166 and supply the audited annual foundation; licensed 8,713.558 plus produced 7,708.608 agrees. The cash note already makes the correct audit distinction.

Correct the caution in a new revision to attribute the audited figure to the 2025 10-K, corroborated by the unaudited Q4 letter. Preserve 16,422.166 and the conditional guidance calculations. Outcome here: open for parent correction; no numerical or economic conclusion changes.
