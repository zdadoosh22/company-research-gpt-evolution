## Conditional valuation and response-price requirements

For a coherent equity earnings method, let E be supportable forward earnings per split-adjusted share after sustainable taxes and ownership effects, and M a separately justified capitalization multiple. Value = M x E. At the dated $71.57 reference, the required E is $71.57/M; this algebra identifies the question without choosing a return hurdle or reverse-fitting M. Operating cash valuation instead requires cash content, cash taxes, capex, working capital, SBC/claims and the debt/interest bridge. No numerical M, discount rate, terminal growth, scenario probabilities or target is invented.

A durable revision must survive the response-price test. If earnings rise from E0 to E1 and the multiple changes from M0 to M1, price support changes in proportion to (E1/E0) x (M1/M0); even correct earnings improvement can be offset by multiple compression. A temporary quarter's cost deferral changes the timing of earnings, not necessarily the recurring E. For a modest days-to-months window, expected revised cash flows can change current value immediately, but a five-year end value cannot be relabelled a near-term target.
