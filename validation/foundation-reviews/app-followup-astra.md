## Coherent minimum construction

1. Use matched latest-disclosed cash and ordinary-share snapshots, or quantify any date mismatch and estimated intervening cash/share movements. Never present the estimate as undisclosed actuals.
2. Forecast recurring operating economics after economic compensation, retaining cloud, R&D and other recurring costs. Exclude finite acquired amortisation once. Broad, justified margin scenarios can replace unavailable detailed cost forecasts; cost sensitivities must reconcile to those margins.
3. Choose one lease/capital convention. A cash-service convention replaces finance-lease depreciation with full lease service, adds owned-asset/licence replacement and growth expenditure, retains operating rent and deducts no additional lease liability. An enterprise approach includes new leased-asset investment in capex and lease debt once in the equity bridge; financing proceeds are not FCFF.
4. Forecast working-capital stocks and charge their changes. Initialise reported narrow receivables minus payables; use a consistent revenue base and justified transition/sensitivity. Keep financing accruals and tax settlements separate.
5. Use contractual coupons plus explicit fees and chosen lease service. Do not also deduct total annualised cash interest for the same obligations. Explicitly repay/refinance the2029 maturity, with rate and fee sensitivity; later debt obligations remain reflected in horizon value.
6. Separate existing awards from future employee compensation. Full option shares require exercise proceeds once; settled awards may overlap outstanding shares. Future cash-equivalent compensation and dilution for the same grants cannot both be charged. Conditional PSUs need scenario/path treatment.
7. Separate conditional value from conviction. Test operating margin, renewal investment, working capital, growth fade and financing, including structural impairment. A price judgement needs to survive material ranges.

Ten forecast years can support a longer transition while retaining the five-year shareholder horizon, but the asset lives do not establish a mandatory ten-year forecast. Show fade-length sensitivity.

The reviewer quantified the scale of several gaps: historical ROU additions plus unpaid licences are aboutUSD0.216/share as a one-time adverse illustration, not a rigorous liability bound; the GAAP-versus-cash interest gap is aboutUSD0.114/share under the stated perpetual assumptions; the gross historical award pool changes the r002 denominator by about0.36%. These do not replace a renewal forecast. Buyback uncertainty must change cash and shares together rather than deducting cash alone.

Disposition: preserve the corrected partial pilot; build and validate a company-specific replacement in a new revision before accepting its numerical conclusions. No identified missing fact inherently prohibits that future conditional construction.
