# APP cash, financing and ownership foundation review

Research boundary: information published by 2026-10-06; prepared 2026-10-07 as an independent pilot repair note. This is not an approved thesis or a fabricated October balance sheet. Existing runs and shared methodology/tools/reports were left unchanged. USD millions and million shares unless stated otherwise.

## Finding and recommended numerical status

Cash, bond principal and contractual coupons can support explicitly dated latest-disclosed calculations. The December outstanding-award pool is small relative to ordinary shares, and the June equity bridge can be audited. An exact October fully diluted ownership schedule is unavailable in the documents reviewed. Replace the existing 335.989m EPS-based proxy with a basic-share snapshot and an explicit gross-award sensitivity, not a claim that the proxy is fully diluted current ownership.

The historical financing foundation is substantially stronger than the r002 limitation implies: the debt coupons and lease schedule are disclosed. But the December lease maturity table is not a current schedule after $60.319m of H1 net ROU additions/modifications. A conditional valuation can use disclosed snapshots, a clearly modelled lease-renewal/cash-service path, and debt maturity/refinancing choices. It must still withhold an unconditional October value/price verdict if missing post-June liquidity/repurchases or the sustainable operating forecast materially affect it. This note does not cure the separate reinvestment/competitive-economics gaps.

Acceptable valuation routes:

1. Do not subtract the finance-lease liability again from this equity valuation.

Reinvestment/renewal economics remain a distinct forecast task. ## Compensation and double-count controls

H1 expense note8 reports169.252 total SBC; CFO adds back168.981 excluding cash-settled awards. Difference=.271. The equity statement records83.372+85.609=168.981. Do not mistake the noncash CFO addback for total compensation expense.

Keep existing outstanding claims separate from future employee compensation. If forward margins deduct a cash-equivalent recurring compensation cost, do not also impose dilution for those same future grants. Alternatively add back the corresponding future noncash SBC and model grant issuance, vesting, withholding and ownership consistently. A zero future share-growth assumption with compensation retained is defensible only as an explicit cash-replacement/economic-cost convention, not as a statement that the company will issue no shares. Existing-award amortization/settlement needs a separate bridge so the same obligation is not charged twice.

Similarly, buybacks lower both cash and claims; do not subtract them as operating expense or add a separate buyback yield to a per-share return already reflecting fewer shares. Financing/lease interest is deducted once, cash is added once, and a direct equity model does not deduct debt/leases again after already charging their service and principal path.

## Precise continuation required for accepted numerical valuation

Obtain or explicitly condition on latest disclosed June cash/July ordinary shares rather than claiming current snapshots. Replace EPS awards with the disclosed-pool sensitivities above. Choose a coherent SBC future-cost versus dilution convention and exercise proceeds. Build an explicit gross maturity/refinancing schedule including2029; choose a lease treatment and justify replacement/renewal cash flow. Reconcile changes sinceJune for any subsequently found material capital event. Quantify resulting per-share and cash-path sensitivities. If a resulting conclusion survives these ranges and operating assumptions, conditional numerics can be useful; neither an exact current balance sheet nor an unqualified current-price verdict follows from this note.
